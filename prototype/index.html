🎓 FPL Analytics Academy: Core Decision Concepts

1. Expected Points (EP)

The mathematical mean of fantasy points a player is projected to score in a gameweek, combining playing time, goal/assist equity, clean sheet odds, and fixture difficulty.

2. Points Per Million (PPM / Value)

The capital efficiency of an asset. Budget enablers scoring 4.5 EP at £5.0m offer a 0.90 PPM, allowing you to fund premium captaincy anchors.

3. Opportunity Cost

The points sacrificed across your remaining 10 starting spots when you tie up £15.5m in a single premium player.

4. Process vs. Outcome

Evaluating players based on underlying repeatable metrics (shots in box, xG, xA) rather than noisy one-off deflections or flukey penalty saves.
